# Sample Data

These small CSVs are included to make the demo data model visible.

- `customer_transactions_sample.csv` mirrors the customer transaction shape of UCI Online Retail: customer, invoice, product, category, purchase date, and channel.
- `digital_events_sample.csv` mirrors ecommerce event datasets such as RetailRocket and OTTO: customer or visitor, event type, item or content, and event count.
- `campaign_history_sample.csv` adds CRM campaign exposure and response, which is needed for the store associate assistant but is not present in the public datasets.

The records are Samsung-flavored sample rows for demonstration. They are not real Samsung customer records.
